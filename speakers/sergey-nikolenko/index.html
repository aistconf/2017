Sergey Nikolenko

JetBrains, Steklov Institute of Math

Hello!

I am a computer scientist residing at St. Petersburg, Russia.

Right now I am most interested in machine learning and network algorithms, but my previous research includes works on cryptography, theoretical computer science, and algebra.

Career

Aug 11, 1984 Born in Pskov, USSR
1991-1994 School №5, Pskov
1994-1998 Pskov Technical Lyceum (graduated in 1998 cum laude)
1998-1999 University High School, Greeley, CO, USA (graduated in 1999 cum laude)
2000-2005 St. Petersburg State University, Department of Mathematics and Mechanics (graduated in 2005 cum laude)
2006-2007 Engineering intern at Google Inc., St. Petersburg.
2005-2008 Graduate student at the Laboratory of Mathematical Logic of the St. Petersburg Department of the Steklov Mathematical Institute
2006-2010 Assistant Professor, SPSU IFMO, St. Petersburg.
2008-2010 Senior Researcher, Speech Technology Center, St. Petersburg.
2011-2012 Senior Researcher at the Bioinformatics Lab of the Academic University.
2011-2014 Research Director, Surfingbird.
2008-present Adjunct Professor, Academic University, St. Petersburg.
2008-present Researcher at the Laboratory of Mathematical Logic of the St. Petersburg Department of the Steklov Mathematical Institute
2013-present Senior Researcher, Laboratory of Internet Studies of the National Research University Higher School of Economics, St. Petersburg.
2015-present Senior Researcher, Deloitte Analytics Institute.
